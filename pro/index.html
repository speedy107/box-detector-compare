    How to get a good reading

    Distance is approximate and measured to the nearest cube surface. Use stationary 35 × 35 × 35 cm cubes with clear, visible edges.

    Once referenced, your viewing angle can change. Use the same camera and zoom; set a new reference after changing either. Check readings against a measured distance.

    Detection runs on your device. Processing time measures the app’s work per frame, not network ping. Phone speed, lighting, blur and hidden corners affect results.
